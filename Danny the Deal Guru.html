JetBlue Adds New Fort Lauderdale Flights to Honduras and Grand Cayman

JetBlue is continuing to expand at Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International Airport (FLL), with flights now on sale for two new daily nonstop international routes to Comayagua, Honduras, home to Palmerola International Airport (XPL), which serves the capital city of Tegucigalpa; and George Town, Grand Cayman, Cayman Islands, via Owen Roberts International Airport (GCM).

Tegucigalpa will be a brand-new destination for JetBlue and its second in Honduras, further strengthening the airline’s growing network across Latin America and the Caribbean.

Flights to Grand Cayman will begin February 23, 2027, followed by Tegucigalpa on February 25. JetBlue will also offer limited-time daily nonstop service between Fort Lauderdale and Port of Spain from January 29 through February 15, giving customers a convenient option to travel to Trinidad and Tobago for the country’s popular Carnival celebrations.
