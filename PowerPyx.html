GTA 6 Sisters of the Sea (Help Free the Orca) Walkthrough

Sisters of the Sea (Help Free the Orca) is a Strangers and Freaks Mission (Side Quest) in GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI). This walkthrough will guide you through all objectives of the Sisters of the Sea (Help Free the Orca) side mission.

  • Starting Location: Vice City > Catalan Key
  • Chapter: Any Chapter
  • Quest Giver: Rosebud (Dodos Rising)
  • Requirement: Give your details to the protesters
  • Can Be Started By: Jason/Lucia
  • Reward: None
  • Quest Description: Rosebud, from the Dodos Rising protest group, wants to free the orca from the Vice City Oceanarium. Meet her there.
  • Questline (Dodos Rising): Bring Out the Chimp > Sisters of the Sea > Twin Flames > Dodo, Rise!

Objectives:

  1. Follow Rosebud
  2. Protect Rosebud
  3. Answer Rosebud
  4. Retrieve the crystal
  5. Follow Rosebud
  6. Steal the truck
  7. Drive the orca to the drop-off

Starting Location: Sisters of the Sea (Help Free the Orca)

To start this mission, you first need to meet the group of protesters that are outside the Vice City Oceanarium in Catalan Key.

Speak with the one on the ground (Rosebud) and agree to give her your details. She will then contact you within a day about their plan to save the orca in the Oceanarium. You can then go to the marked location to the south of the buildings solo as Jason or Lucia to start the mission.


Follow Rosebud

After the initial scene and waiting for nighttime, follow Rosebud as she makes her way into the Oceanarium.

Protect Rosebud

Immediately when you enter the area, you are spotted by security. Go over and melee them to knock them out, then continue following Rosebud.

After seeing Cullen beating up the security guard, keep following Rosebud. You can crouch through the bushes where she directs to avoid being spotted by the other security guards.

Answer Rosebud

After Rosebud “cleanses” the manatee, she wants you to jump into the tank and collect her crystal. You can agree to do this, or decline.

Retrieve the crystal

If you agree, jump into the tank to look for the crystal. It is basically straight down from where you jump in on the floor of the tank. If you are having trouble spotting it you can use Focus + which will highlight it in a brighter color.

Follow Rosebud

You can then climb out of the tank, and Rosebud tells you to keep the crystal. You can then keep following her through the area. Inside she will call out a security guard at some vending machines, you can avoid dealing with them by quickly crouch walking behind them.

Steal the truck

Outside you will find some workers loading the orca into a truck, which Rosebud wants you to steal. You can fire a gun into the air which will immediately scare off all the workers so you don’t need to deal with them.

Drive the orca to the drop-off

You then need to drive the truck to the marked drop-off point in Corsair Key. You will have police called on you on the way, but you can just keep driving and they won’t catch you.

After reaching the drop-off, you will automatically release the orca, completing the mission. You will have the police on you, so should leave the area quickly to escape them. There is no reward from this mission, but you can take Rosebud’s Speedo van while you are leaving if you wanted to keep or sell it.

This finishes Sisters of the Sea (Help Free the Orca) side quest in GTA VI.

For all other Quests refer to the complete GTA 6 Walkthrough.
