Everything You Need to Know for the Lancaster Firetruck Parade

Rob BanksPublished: November 25, 2026
  • Share on Facebook
Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 2:08
Instaread

The Christmasville Firetruck Parade is on the calendar and it is attracting all different fire truck and emergency companies from all over New York State and Pennsylvania and goes right down Central Ave in Lancaster heading toward the Village of Lancaster at 6 PM this coming Saturday!

It is a tradition that has grown bigger and bigger every single year now gearing up for the 19th year. Get your kids bundled up, pour a little mini bottle in that hot cocoa for the parents and head out to Lancaster! What day is the Christmas fire truck parade in Lancaster?

The 19th annual Lancaster Christmasville Fire Truck date is set!

The Greater Lancaster Museum of Firefighting 19th Annual CHRISTMASVILLE FIRE TRUCK PARADE
SATURDAY, NOVEMBER 28, 2025
6pm

If you have never been before, we will help you out!

Where should I stand or bring my chair for the parade?

The parade route is fairy short so, make sure that you get there early to get a spot. The parade goes from the Lancaster High School @ 1 Forton Drive and goes to the Lancaster Opera House @ 21 Central Ave.

The whole route is just over 2 miles.

How long does it last?

They are very much on time at 6 PM and the entire parade usually lasts about an hour.

How can I get my fire truck or company involved in the parade?

To get in, make sure you contact Tom or Bryan at tom@pactoolmounts.com or call the Museum @ 716-206-0925. You can also apply online as well to either be in the parade or a sponsor.

Emergency vehicles from all over Western New York and Pennsylvania come up to participate in the parade. From grinch themed, Charlie Brown you will see some seriously creative scenes come down Central Avenue. The Parade will begin at Lancaster High School 1 Forton Dr. and will continue South on Central Ave. into the Village of Lancaster. Make sure that you get there early though, it fills up fast because it has been getting so popular!
