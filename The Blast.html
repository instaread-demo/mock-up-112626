The song’s visuals, released in July of this year, showcase a satirical piece laced with horror undertones. Grande aimed to highlight the darker side of fame and the public eye while underscoring feminine rage. 

The message of the screen project was, however, not so eagerly received, as concerned fans raised eyebrows at her noticeably thinner appearance, slamming her for promoting an unhealthy body image.

Ariana Grande Finally Addresses ‘Petal’ Music Video Backlash

While on the “Las Culturistas” podcast, Grande opened up about the heated debate that has surrounded her for the past couple of months. 

The singer started by granting some perspective on what the visuals were supposed to portray. She said her character goes through periods where she tries to be malleable and accommodating to whatever is needed of her, before finally coming back to herself at the end, much to her team’s dismay. 

She then stood ten toes down on her creative direction for the production, saying she wouldn’t change a thing about it, before commenting on how the public’s perception of art has changed over the years. 

The former child star rounded up by confirming that her creativity has never been influenced by people’s reactions, and she was proud of how authentic she had remained. In her words:

“As long as I’m creating from an authentic place and being honest and sharing what I know needs to come out and making things that I love…let the chips fall where they may, my people will be my people, and it’s okay if they are not.” 

What Did Fans Have To Say About Ariana Grande’s Observations

Following her response, an excerpt of the clip went viral on X and several social media users were unimpressed, arguing that the Hollywood star was missing the point of the criticism.

They highlighted that they weren’t criticizing her artistic vision, but her disturbing physical appearance in the video.

“The art was not the reason for the backlash. LMAO, she literally got a content warning on her team’s Instagram post for promoting eating disorders,” one fan slammed, before adding, “she can’t stomach any accountability.”

Another user responded, writing, “She [Grande] keeps taking breaks mid-sentences cuz she knows she’s speaking b-llsh-t. You were rightfully dragged for promoting ed.” 

Others felt the “Victorious” actress was shifting attention from her weight by not addressing it directly and instead acting like they were upset about her “changing persona.” 

This disappointed fan wrote, “Now, ari…pls don’t insult our intelligence like this,” while another added, “Why are we pretending to ignore the issue? She is unwell. For her to double down on this so hard should only be more concerning.”

Echoing similar sentiments, another X noted, “It was because of how worringly thin she looked in it, and the fact she’s trying to gaslight the public by saying otherwise is manipulative af.”

Fans Had an Unsettling Reaction to Ariana Grande’s ‘Petal’ Video 

The uproar began in July following the release of the infamous “Petal” video, a single from Grande’s eighth studio album. 

The nearly 7-minute production portrayed Grande looking extremely slim, with her shoulder bones and ribs on full display, immediately catching the attention of her supporters. 

Many were quick to point out how Grande was one of the generation’s biggest talents, lamenting how they were watching her “die right in front of our eyes.” Some noted that she looked “deeply unwell.”

Others saw it as a clarion call for her close friends and family to look out for the Grammy-winning singer, with well-wishers saying they just wanted her to take care of herself without succumbing to beauty standards. 

Ariana Grande Previously Addressed Body Shaming Remarks 

The “Focus” artist is not new to conversations about her body, and followers will recall that in 2023, she finally addressed some of these statements in a rare TikTok. 

At the time, Grande started by acknowledging how unexpected it was for her to be talking about it, as it wasn’t a topic she discussed much.

She then explained that “healthy can look different” for different people, before disclosing that the body fans she championed were among the unhealthiest she has been in her life, as she was drinking and taking antidepressants, among other things. 

The vocalist also reminded people to be kind, as they did not know what anyone was going through in their personal lives, as shared by PEOPLE Magazine.

Ariana Grande Announces Two ‘Eternal Sunshine’ Projects

Away from public commentary, the chart-dominating artist recently made headlines with the announcement of her latest projects. 

Grande is set to release two films documenting her “Eternal Sunshine” tour, per Billboard.

The first will grant fans a behind-the-scenes documentary titled “The Eternal Sunshine Diaries: The Making Of The Tour,” which follows Grande and her team through the 30 days before opening night. It will be released on 11 December. 

The second is a concert film, “The Eternal Sunshine Tour,” out on 12 December, featuring professionally filmed footage of actual tour performances.
