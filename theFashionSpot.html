Gracie Abrams’ Chloe Bodysuit Literally Hangs by the Thinnest Straps

September 28, 2026

By Dolly Bhamrick

Gracie Abrams brought a playful twist to her latest Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon appearance. This time, she traded her polished interview look for something far more unexpected. The singer stepped out in a colorful Chloé bodysuit. But one small detail gave the floral piece a much more daring edge. And Abrams’ decision to pair it with sporty separates only made the styling choice stand out even more.

Gracie Abrams Takes on Jimmy Fallon Show in Chloé Bodysuit Hanging by Just 2 Thin Straps

During her appearance on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon. The singer wore a Chloé Garden Print bodysuit covered in bright pink, yellow, and green floral details. The fitted design featured a sweetheart-style neckline with lace edging and two ultra-thin printed straps running over her shoulders.

The Gracie Abrams Chloe look was styled with black marching-band pants featuring contrasting red and white side stripes. The sporty trousers created a surprising contrast with the romantic floral bodysuit. She added a green Chanel crocodile-embossed leather belt around her waist, while Chanel Camélia Précieux earrings provided a more polished finishing touch.

The combination transformed the floral bodysuit from a traditionally feminine piece into a statement look. The barely-there straps also kept the neckline completely unobstructed, making the colorful Chloé print the main focus of the outfit.

Abrams has played with delicate straps before, making this latest look feel familiar. In 2025, she appeared in a Cosmopolitan shoot wearing a lavender Chanel minidress featuring an empire waist, a plunging neckline, and two barely-there spaghetti straps. The dress also included a floral appliqué at the center of the neckline.

Abrams has also embraced a softer side of Chanel. At the 2025 Grammys, she wore a custom butter-chiffon Chanel gown with tied-off-the-shoulder sleeves, a sheer veil, and a long train, giving an elegant bridal feel. This time, Abrams gave that delicate aesthetic a sportier twist. The floral Chloé bodysuit, relaxed trousers, and barely-there straps made for one of her more unexpected fashion combinations.
