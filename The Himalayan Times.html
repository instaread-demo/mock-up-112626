AmCham Nepal, Niural AI highlight Nepal-US business ties at Thanksgiving gathering

THT Online

By THT Online
Published: 06:40 pm Nov 25, 2026

KATHMANDU, NOVEMBER 25

The American Chamber of Commerce in Nepal (AmCham Nepal) and its member company Niural AI have highlighted growing business, cultural and technological ties between Nepal and the United States at a Thanksgiving gathering in Kathmandu.

The event, titled "Founders' Social: A Thanksgiving Gathering", was held at the Kathmandu Marriott Hotel on November 24, bringing together American business leaders, AmCham members, US Embassy representatives and diplomatic guests.

Addressing the gathering, US Embassy Chargé d'Affaires Megan Bouldin drew parallels between the American Thanksgiving tradition and Nepal's harvest festivals, saying shared cultural values could strengthen people-to-people connections and economic partnerships.

She also acknowledged AmCham Nepal's role in connecting American companies, technology and investment with Nepal and creating employment opportunities for the country's young workforce.

AmCham Nepal Chairperson Kailash Raj Bijayananda reaffirmed the chamber's commitment to policy advocacy, investment facilitation and addressing challenges faced by its members.

A key highlight was a presentation by Niural AI Co-Founder and President Nabin Banskota, who shared the company's journey from Nepal to New York. The AI-powered platform provides global payroll, human resources and employment services to companies in more than 150 countries. It has more than 200 employees across over 10 countries, with its largest team based in Nepal.

Dhiraj Khanal, head of applied AI at Niural AI, also presented the work of Niural AI Labs and how artificial intelligence is transforming global payroll services. Niural APAC General Manager Roshan Lama delivered the closing remarks.

Nepal's ambassador-designate to the United States, Chiran Jung Thapa, also attended the gathering, alongside representatives of the US Embassy.

AmCham Nepal, which has 72 member companies across sectors including information technology, energy, tourism, manufacturing and services, works to promote commercial relations and investment between Nepal and the United States.
